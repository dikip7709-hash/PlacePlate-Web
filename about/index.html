Our Story

Built around the power of visual storytelling

PlacePlate was founded with one belief: every great idea deserves a presentation that does it justice. We saw too many brilliant concepts get lost behind cluttered slides and inconsistent design — so we decided to change that.

Starting as a small creative project, we grew into a trusted presentation studio serving clients from all over the world — from startup founders pitching to investors, to academics defending their thesis, to corporate teams presenting quarterly results.
